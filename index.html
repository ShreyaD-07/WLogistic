About WithX

We move the world’s freight as one.

WithX is a unified logistics company. Road, sortation, ocean, and air share a single operating picture — so a carton scanned in Pune is already a slot on a vessel, a truck, or a cargo jet.

What we do

We run hubs that never sleep, a national India corridor, and gateways to sea and sky. Control rooms watch telemetry from orbit to dock door. People, robots, and fleet captains work from the same live map.

How we think

Logistics is not a stack of vendors. It is one motion: identify, route, hand off, prove. If a lane fails, the network rebalances. If a window opens, freight takes it.

Where we operate

Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Chennai, and Kolkata form the inland graph. Ports and cargo aircraft extend that graph overnight. The same WithX identity follows every consignment.
